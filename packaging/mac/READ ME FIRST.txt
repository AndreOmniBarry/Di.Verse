Di.Verse for Mac
================

1. Drag Di.Verse.app into your Applications folder.

2. Open it the first time (only once):
   Double-click Di.Verse. macOS says it "cannot verify the developer"
   (Di.Verse isn't registered with Apple yet). Click Done, then:
     System Settings → Privacy & Security → scroll down →
     "Di.Verse was blocked…" → Open Anyway → enter your password.
   (Older macOS: right-click Di.Verse → Open → Open.)
   If it still won't open, run this once in Terminal:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Di.Verse.app

3. The Operator page opens in Google Chrome (needed for the fastest,
   live-caption listening). Click Settings (top right) and paste your free
   Groq and Gemini keys. Already used the Terminal version? Your keys,
   Bibles and songs from Desktop/Di.Verse are brought across automatically.

From then on: open Di.Verse from Applications or the Dock. It runs in the
background; Settings → Stop Di.Verse stops it.

Your keys, Bibles and songs: ~/Library/Application Support/Di.Verse
(kept when you install a newer version: just replace the app).
vMix: http://<this Mac's IP>:3000/projection?overlay=1
